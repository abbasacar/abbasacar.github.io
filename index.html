Abbas Acar

Senior Security Researcher

Geotab

I am a Senior Security Researcher at Geotab, working on product security. Previously, I led medical device security engagements at Harbor Labs. I received my PhD from Florida International University (FIU), where I also worked as a Postdoctoral Associate. My research has appeared at NDSS, USENIX Security, and ACM Computing Surveys.

I tend to look at whole systems rather than single components. Many of the serious issues I’ve found weren’t in the cryptography or the protocol itself but in how the pieces fit together: a hardened device with its private key sitting in readable flash, a mutually authenticated channel that faithfully delivers a malicious update, a sound AES-GCM design undone by nonce reuse. Those seams are where I spend most of my time.
