Unveiling the Global Landscape of Android Security Updates
Research overview This follow-up to our NDSS Android study analyzes 567,000 security-update records from 2014 to 2024, covering 904 devices from six OEMs across 98 countries. We compare update timing across manufacturers, devices, and regions, examine documented vulnerabilities and OEM compliance with Android security guidelines, and identify gaps in patch availability and support.
Haiyun Deng, Güliz Seray Tuncay, Abbas Acar, Esteban Luques, Harun Oz, Ahmet Aris, Selcuk Uluagac
